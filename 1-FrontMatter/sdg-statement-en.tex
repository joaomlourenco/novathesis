% This particular text explains why the novathesis template itself
% contributes to SDGs 4, 9, 10 and 17 (set in 0-Config/1_novathesis.tex).
% If you change print/sdgs/list for your own document, rewrite this text
% to justify your own selection instead.

As a free, open-source template, \novathesis{} itself contributes to several of these goals: it lowers a real barrier between students and completing their degree across dozens of schools and twenty languages (SDG~4, Quality Education); being free and open removes the cost of paid formatting tools or services, extending that access to students who could not otherwise afford it (SDG~10, Reduced Inequalities); it is reusable open infrastructure that other institutions build their own configurations on top of (SDG~9, Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure); and it is maintained through public contributions, a per-school maintainer program, and open community discussion (SDG~17, Partnerships for the Goals).
